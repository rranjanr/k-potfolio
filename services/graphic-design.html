How I approach design for Nepali businesses

Most of the people who see your posts are on a phone, scrolling fast, often on mobile data. So I design for that: big readable text, one clear message per post, product photos that are sharp, and file sizes that load quickly.

I also design with the Nepali calendar in mind. A lot of engagement for local businesses comes around festivals, and a greeting that goes up late or looks like a generic template does little for your brand. I plan those posts ahead so they are ready and consistent with the rest of your page.

Language matters too. Many of my posts mix Nepali and English, because that is how customers in Biratnagar and Kathmandu actually read and write. I write or check the Nepali text myself so the message is correct, not just translated.
