Why hire one person for design, content and ads?

A lot of Nepali businesses split their social media between a designer who makes posts, a relative who uploads them, and an agency that boosts them. Each person sees only part of the picture, so the page looks inconsistent, messages go unanswered, and nobody can say which boost actually brought customers.

When the same person designs the post, writes the caption, runs the ad and replies to the people who message, the whole thing fits together. I know which product the ad is pushing because I made the creative, and I know which ads are working because I am the one reading the leads in the inbox.

That is how I work today for Rouniyar International and Bath Nepal: product posts and reels, festival greetings, scripts, AI-generated video, Meta ads and lead replies, all handled together.

FAQ

Questions about my services

Which service should I start with?

If your page is inactive, start with social media management so you have steady content. If your page already posts regularly but brings few enquiries, start with Meta ads. If you only need designs that your own team will post, choose graphic design.

Do you offer monthly packages?

Yes. Most clients book a monthly package that combines design, posting, reels and ads. Each package is quoted to your goals rather than taken from a fixed price list.

Can you also build a website?

Yes. I trained as a UI/UX designer and front-end developer, and can design and build a landing page or small website for your ads to send people to. See the web projects in my portfolio.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a week of agreeing on the plan. Festival campaigns can be prepared sooner if the date is close.

How do we communicate?

Mostly on WhatsApp, with a call or an in-person meeting at the start. You approve content through a shared calendar.

Not sure which service you need?

Send me your Facebook or Instagram page and I will tell you what I would fix first.
