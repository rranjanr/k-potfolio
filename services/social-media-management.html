Why consistency beats going viral

Most small business pages in Nepal post in bursts: five posts in one week, then nothing for a month. The algorithm and your customers both notice. A page that posts steadily, answers messages quickly and looks the same every time earns trust, and when someone finally needs a pump, a sofa or a haircut, they remember who kept showing up.

That is why my management work is built around a calendar and a routine, not one-off posts.

FAQ

Social media management questions

What is included in social media management?

A monthly content calendar, post and story design, reels and video editing, captions and scripts, posting on schedule, replies to comments and messages, and a monthly report. Meta ads can be added to any package.

How many posts will you make each month?

That depends on your package. Many businesses do well with three to five posts a week plus a few reels. I recommend a number after looking at your page and your competitors.

Which platforms do you manage?

Mainly Facebook and Instagram, which is where most Nepali customers are. The short videos I make can also be reused on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

Do you reply to messages from customers?

Yes, when it is part of your package. I answer common questions about products, prices and availability and pass serious buyers to you.

How much does social media management cost in Nepal?

Packages are quoted per business, based on the number of posts and reels, whether ads are included and how much inbox handling you need. Send your page link for a quote.

Do I need to send product photos?

It helps. You can send photos and short clips from your phone and I edit them into posts and reels. When there is no footage, I use design and AI-generated video.
