Why hire a remote social media manager in Kathmandu?

Kathmandu has no shortage of digital marketing agencies, but many small businesses there run into the same problems: a monthly retainer that is hard to justify, a different person answering every time you call, and posts that look like they were made for someone else's brand.

Working with me means one person owns your page. I design the posts, write the captions, edit the reels, run the ads and answer the messages. You always know who to talk to, and nothing gets lost between departments.

Social media work does not need an office visit. Everything happens on your page, in Meta Business Suite and in a shared folder, which is exactly how I already work day to day.

How remote collaboration works

  • Kick-off call on WhatsApp or Google Meet to understand your business, products and customers
  • Partner access through Meta Business Suite, so you never share your password
  • Shared calendar so you can approve posts before they go live
  • Product photos and videos sent from your phone to a shared folder; I turn them into posts and reels
  • WhatsApp group for quick approvals, offers and questions
  • Monthly report with reach, messages, leads and ad spend

Services for Kathmandu Valley businesses

  • Social media management for Facebook and Instagram, including reels and inbox replies
  • Meta ads and Facebook boosting targeted to Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur, or specific areas within the valley
  • Graphic design for posts, festival greetings, logos, menus and ad creatives

Ads targeted inside the valley

Kathmandu Valley is crowded with advertisers, so broad boosts waste money fast. I narrow targeting to the areas and people who can actually reach you, for example a radius around your restaurant or clinic, and set campaigns up for messages so you get real enquiries instead of only likes.

Questions from Kathmandu businesses

Can you manage my page if I am in Kathmandu and you are in Biratnagar?

Yes. Social media work happens online: I get partner access to your page, you send product photos and videos, and we talk on WhatsApp and calls.

How do I approve posts?

Through a shared content calendar, with quick approvals on WhatsApp. Nothing goes live that you have not seen, unless you want me to post freely.

Can you target ads to specific areas in Kathmandu Valley?

Yes. Ads can target Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur separately, or a radius around your business.

Which Kathmandu businesses do you work with?

Restaurants and cafes, clinics, salons, retail shops, training centers, distributors and online sellers: any business whose customers are on Facebook and Instagram.

How quickly can we start?

Usually within a week of the kick-off call, once page access and the first content plan are ready.
