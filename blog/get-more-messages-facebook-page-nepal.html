How to Get More Messages from Your Facebook Page in Nepal

By Vaishali Sharma, graphic designer and social media marketer in Biratnagar · 6 min read · September 27, 2026

Quick answer: to get more messages from your Facebook page, post consistently with clear product and price information, add a "Send message" or WhatsApp button, end every post with a simple call to action, run message ads targeted to your area, and reply within minutes, not hours. Fast replies turn messages into sales more than anything else.

For most businesses in Nepal, Facebook is not about likes. It is about the inbox. A customer sees a post, sends "price?", and the sale happens in chat. So the real goal of your page is simple: more good messages, answered well.

These are the steps I use on the brand accounts I manage, where I handle both the content and the replies to the leads that come in.

1. Fix your page basics first

  • Profile picture: your logo, readable at small size.
  • Cover image: what you sell and where, for example "Water pumps and motors, wholesale and retail, Biratnagar".
  • Action button: set it to Send message or WhatsApp, not "Follow".
  • About section: location, phone, opening hours and delivery areas.
  • Instant reply and FAQs: set up automatic answers in Meta Business Suite for common questions like location and hours.

2. Post what makes people ask

Posts that only say "Happy Friday" do not bring enquiries. Posts that show a product, a benefit and a reason to ask do.

  • Product posts with a clear photo, key details and "Message us for price"
  • Short reels showing the product in use
  • Offers with a deadline
  • Customer questions turned into posts ("Which pump for a 3-storey house?")
  • Behind the scenes of your shop or warehouse, which builds trust

3. End every post with one clear action

Tell people exactly what to do: "Message us for today's price", "WhatsApp us for dealer rates", "Comment 'info' and we will message you". One action per post works better than three.

4. Post consistently

A page that posts three to five times a week, every week, gets more messages than a page that posts ten times one week and then disappears for a month. Plan a simple calendar so you are never wondering what to post today. Include the festivals, which are your easiest high-engagement days. See my festival content plan for Nepali businesses.

5. Run message ads in your area

Organic reach on Facebook is limited, so a small, steady ad budget helps. Set up ads with the goal of messages, target the city or radius where your customers are, and use your best-performing product post or reel as the ad. For the difference between boosting and Ads Manager, read Facebook Boost vs Meta Ads Manager.

6. Reply fast, and reply well

This is where most businesses lose customers. Someone messages at 8 pm asking for a price, gets a reply the next afternoon, and has already bought somewhere else.

  • Reply within minutes during business hours
  • Give the price or a clear range when asked. "Inbox" as a reply to "price?" frustrates people.
  • Ask one follow-up question ("Which area are you in?") to keep the chat going
  • Share a phone number or WhatsApp for serious buyers
  • Use saved replies in Meta Business Suite for common answers

7. Answer comments publicly

Every comment you answer shows the next visitor that the business is active. Reply to questions in the comments and invite them to message for details.

8. Track what brings messages

Once a month, look at which posts and ads brought the most messages, and which messages turned into sales. Make more of what works. In Meta Business Suite you can see message counts by post and by ad.

Checklist

  • Action button set to Send message or WhatsApp
  • Location, hours and contact in the About section
  • Three to five posts a week, with a clear call to action
  • At least one reel a week
  • Message ads targeted to your area
  • Replies within minutes, with real answers
  • A monthly look at what worked
