About Me

About Vaishali Sharma, Graphic Designer & Social Media Marketer

I'm a graphic designer and social media marketer from Biratnagar, Nepal. I design the posts, make the reels, run the ads and answer the messages, so a business's social media works as one thing instead of four separate jobs.

My Story

From interface design to social media that sells

I have more than a year of graphic design experience, working mostly in Canva, Adobe Illustrator and Figma on social media posts, brand visuals, 3D product graphics and ad creatives. Before that, I trained as a UI/UX designer, and I hold a BCA (Bachelor in Computer Application) from Tribhuvan University (2020 to 2025).

For the last three months I have been working hands-on in digital marketing for Rouniyar International and Bath Nepal. The job covers everything a small business needs from social media: content creation, video editing, AI video generation, script writing, running Meta ads, replying to the leads those ads bring in, and keeping up engagement on both accounts.

My UI/UX internship at Clove I.T. Pvt. Ltd. (2024 to 2025) still shapes how I work. I designed six responsive web and mobile interfaces there, built a component library of 20+ reusable elements, and ran usability tests that cut a checkout and sign-in flow from five steps to three. That habit of watching what real people actually do, and fixing what slows them down, is the same one I use when I look at which posts and ads bring messages and which ones get ignored.

I write in Nepali and English, I plan around the festival calendar, and I work with businesses in person around Biratnagar and remotely across Kathmandu and the rest of Nepal.
