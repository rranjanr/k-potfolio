My Story

From coursework to shipped components

I'm a UI/UX designer and front-end developer based in Biratnagar, Nepal, and a BCA (Bachelor in Computer Application) graduate of Tribhuvan University (2020–2025). My process is shaped less by theory and more by shipping. I spend as much time checking how a component actually behaves in the browser as I do refining it in Figma, which is the same approach I walk through in my design process breakdown.

During my UI/UX design internship at Clove I.T. Pvt. Ltd. (2024–2025), I designed six responsive web and mobile interfaces from wireframe through to high-fidelity Figma file, and built out a component library of 20+ reusable elements so the three-member dev team I worked alongside could ship consistent UI without re-solving the same button, form, and card patterns every sprint. I also planned and ran usability testing sessions that cut the checkout and sign-in flows from five steps down to three, a change that came directly from watching real users get stuck and asking why. That same instinct for cutting friction shows up again in the sign-in flow for Streets to Runways.

Outside of product interfaces, I design brand and social content in Canva, edit and assemble short-form video in CapCut, and have been using AI-assisted tools (including Google's Omni model) to speed up video content generation. Different medium, same goal: making something look intentional and load fast, whether it's a landing page or a 15-second reel.
