FAQ

Common questions from Nepali businesses

Do you only work with businesses in Biratnagar?

No. I am based in Biratnagar and meet clients in person around Biratnagar, Itahari, Dharan and nearby towns, but I also manage pages remotely for businesses in Kathmandu and the rest of Nepal.

How much does social media management cost?

It depends on how many posts and reels you need each month, whether ads and inbox replies are included, and your ad budget. Send me your page link and goals and I will send a quote within 1 to 2 business days.

Can you run Facebook boosting and Meta ads for my page?

Yes. I run simple boosts and full Meta Ads Manager campaigns for messages, leads and website visits, and I reply to the leads they bring in. See Meta ads and Facebook boosting.

Do you write captions and posts in Nepali?

Yes. I write captions, reel scripts and festival greetings in Nepali, English or a mix of both, depending on who your customers are.

Do I have to give you my Facebook password?

No. I work through partner access in Meta Business Suite, so your page and ad account stay yours and you can remove my access at any time.
